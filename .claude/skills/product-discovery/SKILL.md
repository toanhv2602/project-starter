# Product Discovery

1. Ask about target users, pain points, current alternatives and desired outcome.
2. Separate facts, assumptions and open questions.
3. Define primary user journeys and business outcomes.
4. Write functional requirements and non-functional constraints.
5. Prioritize must-have MVP scope; explicitly list out-of-scope work.
6. Write testable acceptance criteria and edge cases.
7. Do not invent a product domain when the owner has not supplied one.
8. Save decisions in `docs/product/` and record unresolved decisions.
